Source: Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, 2025 Survey of Consumer Finances (released Oct 9, 2026). All figures in 2025 dollars, by age of the family's reference person. Median and mean: SCF historical tables (public data), Table 4. Percentiles: computed by @rogueiq from the Fed's 2025 SCF Summary Extract using the survey weights (the same method reproduces the Fed's Table 4 medians and means). The Fed does not publish percentile cut-offs by age. The SCF excludes the Forbes 400. "Compare against" filters (couple/single, kids, home, education, 2024 income, work status) are also computed by @rogueiq from the Summary Extract. Groups with fewer than 100 survey families are widened or not shown. Official all-ages figures in the footnotes are from Table 4.
