Net worth for families aged 45–54 (Fed 2025 data)

Median net worth for U.S. families aged 45–54 was $253,700 in 2025, down 6% from $270,600 in 2022 in inflation-adjusted terms, according to the Federal Reserve’s 2025 Survey of Consumer Finances (released Oct 9, 2026).
